Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Page Audit is a browser extension that audits web pages for performance, accessibility, SEO, and UX issues. Your privacy matters to us.

Data collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The extension runs entirely in your browser on pages you choose to audit.

What the extension does

  • Analyzes the current webpage's DOM, styles, and performance metrics locally
  • Runs accessibility checks using the bundled axe-core library
  • Displays results in the extension popup
  • Highlights elements on the page when you click an issue

Permissions

  • activeTab — Accesses the page you're viewing only when you click the extension
  • scripting — Injects the audit script only when you request an audit

Third-party services

The extension does not connect to external servers. All analysis happens locally.
